## 🏗️ Technical Architecture

### 1. Multi-Lingual Voice Intelligence
*   **Whisper-V3 Integration**: Supports 99+ languages, including **English, Urdu, and Roman-Urdu**.
*   **Speech-to-Text**: High-precision voice recognition using **Whisper-large-v3** for a seamless user experience.

### 2. The Agentic Workflow
*   **📄 NEXUS (The Auditor)**: Performs a deep-dive technical audit of your CV to identify core strengths and critical skill gaps.
*   **🎯 PATHFINDER (The Scout)**: Scans industries to identify specific companies where your hiring probability is highest.
*   **🧠 ZEN (The Warrior Mindset)**: Delivers high-intensity motivation based on historical legends like Thomas Edison to build mental resilience against rejections.
*   **📅 ORBIT (The Strategist)**: Generates a comprehensive 6-month tactical execution plan to guide your career progress.
